On the water, and all around it

Questions guests ask

How much is a room?

The room is from €110 to €160 a night depending on the room, plus breakfast at €12 per person: from €134 to €184 for two, breakfast included (from €122 for one person). Seven rooms sleep three to six, from €120 to €240 a night, so from €156 to €288 with breakfast. The price shown when you book includes breakfast. 15% off from 6 nights. Baby cots are free. Tourist tax: €1.50 per person per night. Every room is described on the rooms page.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Baby cots are free.

Are the rooms air-conditioned?

No. The château sits at about 500 m above sea level by the lake, so nights are cooler than down in the valley.

Where can we hire gear or take a kite, wing or paddleboard lesson?

A watersports school with gear hire is right next to the château.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 4 pm, check-out by 11 am. Rooms are cleaned every three days, or more often if you ask.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, for €12 a night.

Is there parking?

Yes, about 40 free spaces on site, with no EV charging point.
