A summer by the lake, in a château.

Wing foiling in the afternoon, paddleboarding at sunrise, a drink at the guinguette when you get back. In July and August the château opens to everyone: hotel, restaurant and guinguette.

The hotel

13 bedrooms, each one different. Rooms from €110 a night for two, breakfast €12.

  • Gear room with a water point and drying rack
  • Eat early or late, whenever you get back
  • Local tips from people who live here

The restaurant

A short, honest menu built on local produce, by our chef. Served on the terrace, facing the lake.

  • Covered terrace seating 40
  • Picnic baskets for your day out
  • Service that’s there when you need it

The guinguette

An open-air bar under the trees. You order at the bar, pick your spot and stay as long as you like.

  • Music and games, free to use
  • Hammocks, deckchairs, a reading corner

On the water, and all around it

Monteynard is a renowned wind spot. In the afternoon the lake fills with kites, wings and sails; in the morning it belongs to paddleboarders.

  • Wing foiling

  • Windsurfing

Working while you’re away? Reliable wifi, quiet places to work and a spot for your video calls.

Questions guests ask

How much is a room?

From €110 to €160 a night for one or two people, depending on the room; breakfast is €12 per person. Several rooms sleep three to five, from €140 to €240. Tourist tax: €1.50 per person per night.

Are children welcome?

Yes, and we have cots.

Are the rooms air-conditioned?

No. The château sits at about 500 m above sea level by the lake, so nights are cooler than down in the valley.

Where can we hire gear or take a kite, wing or paddleboard lesson?

A watersports school with gear hire is right next to the château.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3 pm to 7 pm, check-out by 11 am.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, for €12 a night.

Is there parking?

Yes, about 40 free spaces on site, with no EV charging point.
