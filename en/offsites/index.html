Questions organisers ask

How many people does each room hold?

Each of the three 60 m² drawing rooms seats about 50 theatre-style, 24 classroom-style and 20 in a U-shape or around a boardroom table. The 220 m² vaulted cellars take the whole group, up to 100 people, for a plenary, a dinner or a standing reception.

What equipment is provided?

A projector with sound, a screen in some rooms (elsewhere you project onto a white wall), flipcharts and fibre wifi. No PA system or video-conferencing kit.

Can you arrange transfers?

Yes, just ask: we’ll look into it and suggest an option from Monestier-de-Clermont station or from Grenoble.

Can you organise a team-building activity?

Yes, on the water and around the lake: sailing, paddleboarding, the Himalayan footbridges, a bike treasure hunt, an evening in the vaulted cellars. Tell us what you have in mind: we suggest a programme and add it to your quote.

Is there parking?

Yes, about 40 free spaces on site. There is no EV charging point.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, during offsites too.
