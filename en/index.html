Barefoot on a château lawn.

In the morning the lake is glassy and belongs to the paddleboarders. In the afternoon the wind picks up and the sails come out. In the evening you dine under beamed ceilings or out on the terrace, facing the mountains.

In between: hammocks, a lawn that runs down to the water, and nobody giving you a look if you turn up in sportswear.
